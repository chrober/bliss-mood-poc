## Source grouping, fit and validation controls

One deterministic set per target scenario, using the P3 fiction generator: 48 wholly separate artist/album groups x six fictional 23D tracks = 288 tracks, eight ordered fictional triplet judgments per group = **384 triplets**. The [existing connected-component split](../scripts/research_grouped_split.py) partitions triplets and track identities together by shared artist/album/recording/triplet family. A nested fixed partition provides:

| Partition | Fictional triplet judgments | Fictional groups | Use |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Training | 192 | 24 | Fit only model parameters, 100 fixed training judgments |
| Calibration | 48 | 6 | Fit pairwise distance-to-common-score mapping only |
| Validation | 48 | 6 | Fixed prospective-style engineering model/calibration gate; no test labels |
| Sealed test | 96 | 12 | Report outcomes after all mapping/model/gate decisions |

Each six-song group has 15 unique unordered pairs. The calibration, validation and test pair datasets therefore contain **90 / 90 / 180 distinct pair rows**, respectively. Pair labels are generated by a fixed artificial oracle of pair **squared distance** at a predefined scalar threshold (20 for axis_sparse, 70 for cross_feature); these are **not** human listening or general musical similarity annotations.

The only General-control model is synthetic 23D identity Euclidean (not the eventual WP1 General Metric). The two fixed Personal-capacity models are the same previously defined positive diagonal or strictly PSD diagonal+rank2 Mahalanobis; their weights are fit on the first 100 train-only ordered fictional triplets using the unchanged fixed P3 learning procedure. The actual matrix weights are **not serialized/exported**.

General and both Personal models each receive a separately fitted, **pair-only, query-independent monotone logistic calibrator** targeting the **same** artificial absolute pair-closeness label, using only the 90 calibration pairs. A versioned frozen mapping uses a calibration-partition-only median distance scale, intercept, nonnegative slope, 600 iterations and frozen regularization. No per-query candidate-set normalization; the score of a songpair is deterministic given its two vectors and frozen mapping. This is only approximate calibration under its fictional distribution and artificially chosen target, *not proof that range mapping alone makes real music scores commensurate*. Neither calibrator parameters nor vectors are published; the same versioned identity digests are used to exercise the compatibility tag.

Validation gates are fixed in code, separately from the TEST answers: both pair classes represented, at least 60 validation pairs, Brier strictly below 0.25, five-bin ECE strictly below 0.25, and Personal must beat General on the same **48 validation triplets**. Only then is a fictional PersonalEvidence flag enabled for the synthetic test; otherwise existing [full-query General fallback](../scripts/general_personal_blend_contract.py) is mandatory. TEST judgments are not consulted for fitting, calibration, threshold selection, gating, or method preference. The five predeclared settings are 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% Personal.

Both Personal candidates passed the same fictional criteria. Each scenario has separate fake oracle labels; do **not** average their scores.

### Triplet ranking, heldout accuracy on 96 fictional judgments

| Fictional target | Model used for calibrated Personal | 0% | 25% | 50% | 75% | 100% |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Axis-aligned sparse | Diagonal | 46.88% | 66.67% | 71.88% | 78.12% | **79.17%** |
| Axis-aligned sparse | Diagonal+rank2 | 46.88% | 63.54% | 63.54% | 65.62% | 65.62% |
| Cross-feature interaction | Diagonal | 50.00% | 58.33% | 61.46% | **62.50%** | 60.42% |
| Cross-feature interaction | Diagonal+rank2 | 50.00% | 56.25% | 58.33% | 58.33% | 57.29% |

Triplet ties count as incorrect, all blends share the same 96 synthetic evaluation questions, and all transforms are globally fixed for a model. The cross-feature rank2 candidate has **better TEST pair Brier** (0.1732 vs 0.2032), yet a **lower TEST triplet accuracy** (57.29% vs 60.42% at 100%). Different objectives measure different properties. This is NOT evidence for selecting either Personal model, tuning to the sealed test outcomes, or activating 75% blend for real music. All numbers are constructed synthetic truth.

The General/Personal compatibility/fallback branch, including model absence, uncertified validation or incompatible profile/model/feature/cohort score contracts, is already tested by the [existing WP2-P0 contract suite](../tests/test_general_personal_blend_contract.py). P4 additionally tests 0/25/50/75/100 query-wide fallback to **exact unchanged General scores** when its local validation switch is false, and verifies identity/source-group isolation, test-label noninterference in fitting/calibration, fixed scorer orientation and reproducible bytes across platforms. A passing *fictional* gate cannot certify an actual person's calibrated Personal Metric: no runtime activation is authorized.

## Decision and next actual research gate

WP2-P4 is an **engineering validation of the architecture**, not independent real musical quality or a claim that Personal is ready for Lyrion. In a real rollout, the necessary source/rights/identity checks and permissions for per-user labeled triplets, adequate user-level and group-disjoint validation, repeated-question reliability, unchanged General reference, fixed calibration target and cross-user isolation must be established separately. Real adaptation must never update/overwrite original `bliss.db`, original feature sources or the selected General scorer; no compulsory new listening is required for ongoing research.

The **WP1 General Metric remains the mandatory primary product and unresolved research goal**. [WP1-E1](wp1-e1-verified-public-benchmark-evidence-20261030.md) independently audited existing historical MTT human / FMA VALIDATION genre / DEAM VA evidence. [WP1-E2](wp1-e2-mtt-source-and-cache-diagnostics-20261030.md) confirmed the publisher 30-clip repeated-range study stopped at 7/30, so no legitimate 1,019-clip global fusion real replay has been completed. Keep FMA official TEST unused and WP3-R6/R7 as teacher-proxy-only evidence. No changes to PR #16, main, production Lyrion, BlissMixerLab or the external Rust learner.
